## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a final-year **B.Tech. (Honors) in Computer Science and Engineering** student at **Prasad V Potluri Siddhartha Institute of Technology** (affiliated to JNTU Kakinada), graduating in 2027, with a **CGPA of 9.59 / 10** and a top-3 rank in my department.

My interest in AI began with the mathematics behind it, and it has since moved from *training* models to *understanding* them: why they predict what they do, and when they should say "I don't know."
